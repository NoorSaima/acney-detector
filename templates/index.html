  • What types of acne can your system detect?

    Our AI system is trained to detect 25 different acne types, including: Blackhead, Whitehead, Papule, Pustule, Nodule, Cyst, Milia, Comedonal, Hormonal, Cystic, Inflammatory, Noninflammatory, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Fungal Acne, Rosacea, Perioral Dermatitis, Steroid Acne, Excoriated Acne, Acne Vulgaris, Acne Conglobata, Acne Fulminans, Pomade Acne, and Mechanical Acne. This helps ensure a complete and accurate analysis for users of all skin types and conditions.

  • Can I track changes in my acne over time using this system?

    Yes, our system allows you to upload updated images over time, making it easier to monitor progress. You can see how your acne severity changes—whether it's improving or worsening—and adjust your skincare routine accordingly.

  • What kind of skincare recommendations will I get?

    Based on your acne type and severity, our AI provides personalized skincare recommendations. This may include product suggestions, routine tips, and lifestyle changes to help reduce acne and improve skin health. The recommendations are designed to support both prevention and treatment.

  • Is my photo data safe and private?

    Yes, your uploaded image is only used for instant acne detection and is not saved or stored anywhere. We do not use your image for training, sharing, or any other purpose. Once the result is shown, the image is automatically discarded.

We help individuals and skincare professionals detect and understand acne types with AI-powered accuracy. Our goal is to make skin analysis fast, reliable, and accessible to everyone.
